## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### **QR Code Not Scanning**:
- **Increase Brightness** of your screen
- **Clean Phone Camera** lens
- **Hold Steady** at 6-12 inches distance
- **Ensure Good Lighting** in the room

### **Link Not Working**:
- **Check URL** in browser address bar
- **Verify App** is running on `yarn start`
- **Refresh Page** and try scanning again
- **Try Different** QR scanner app

### **Modal Not Showing**:
- **Clear Browser Cache** and localStorage
- **Open Incognito Tab** and try again
- **Check Console** for JavaScript errors
- **Restart App** with `yarn start`
